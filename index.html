About us

Flying Dutchman Studios is an independent studio in the Netherlands. We build for the moment a parent hands the phone to a child, so we keep our apps small, quiet and safe: a parent fills everything in, nothing is asked of the child, and no personal data is sold or shared.

Everything is made in house, from the writing and the artwork to the voices.
