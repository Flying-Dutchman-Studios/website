One minute of work, one moment they never forget

You do everything beforehand. Your child only has to pick up.

  1. 1

    Tell him what he knows

    The name, a compliment, a little task, and anything else worth mentioning. Every field is optional.

  2. 2

    Listen before they do

    Play the whole call back first. Change a word, pick another moment in the story, or start again. No surprises.

  3. 3

    Hand over the phone

    It rings for real. Your child answers, hears their own name, and can even answer him back.

About the studio

Flying Dutchman Studios is an independent studio in the Netherlands. Everything is made in house: the writing, the artwork and the voices. We keep our apps small and quiet on purpose. A parent fills everything in, nothing is asked of the child, and no personal data is sold or shared.
