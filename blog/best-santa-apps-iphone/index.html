Best Santa and Christmas apps
for iPhone in 2026

From a personal call from Santa to the NORAD tracker and the family wish list. What is actually worth installing on an iPhone this December, what it costs, and what to check before you hand over the phone.

1A phone call from Santa

The category that lands hardest, because it speaks to your child instead of to the occasion. Five of them are worth knowing about, and they differ mainly in how much you decide.

Ours

Call from Santa

Santa calls your child personally. He says their name, he knows what is on the wish list, and he knows what they have been up to that week.

You put the call together yourself: for every part you pick from suggestions or write your own. You listen to the whole message back before your child hears it, and you decide when the phone rings: now, with thirty seconds to get the handset ready, or later that evening.

First three messages free, then 3 for €1.99, 7 for €2.99 or 15 for €3.99. No subscription and no forced ads. We never ask for a photo of your child.

Full disclosure: we make this one, a small team working evenings. That is why it is flagged rather than quietly slipped in at number one.

See Call from Santa

Portable North Pole

The biggest by far: ten million downloads and a 4.6 average. More than forty scenarios, over fifty scenes, bedtime audio stories, and a live “Talk to Santa” with up to ten personalised exchanges. Their help centre states they run no third-party advertising, which not everyone can say. Two things to know: they publish no price anywhere on their own site, and their store listing says Santa uses your child’s name, age and photo.

Video Call Santa

Santa on screen, 4.7 from 160,000 ratings on iPhone. You choose from 46 pre-recorded episodes and pick a name from a list of 7,000. Individual names cost $1.99, so an unusual name or a second child is a separate purchase. The iPhone version has not had a feature update since November 2024.

Speak to Santa

Video and voice calls that work hard not to feel canned: their own description calls it “dynamic, varied, and realistic” rather than a recording. Watch the pricing, though. The same purchase shows up at two different amounts across their listings, and the separate Pro Edition app costs $39.99 to download while it has not been updated since November 2021.

Message from Santa

The widest feature set for the least money: video, simulated call, voicemail and chat, with a yearly plan at $9.99. It is also the one with ads on both platforms and an in-app purchase literally called “Remove Ads”. The videos are a choice of three ready-made ones with your child’s name and photo added, and the chat is, in their words, “simulated and powered by AI”.

On the left you put the message together and listen back, on the right the phone rings.

2Tracking Santa on Christmas Eve

The NORAD Santa Tracker has been running since 1955, it is free, and it has no catch. Put it on around five o’clock on the 24th and let the children check between courses.

It also hands you the only bedtime argument that has ever worked: he will not come while you are awake.

3Putting Santa in your living room

Capture The Magic drops a Santa into a photo of your own hallway, with almost a hundred characters to choose from. Worth knowing before you install it: it carries a 16+ age rating, unlike everything else here, it has not been updated since December 2023, and its privacy label lists your photos under third-party advertising purposes.

4Christmas music and the countdown

You do not need a dedicated app. Apple Music and Spotify both run Christmas stations from late November. Download one playlist for offline use, because the weakest link in Christmas Eve is the wifi at someone else’s house.

5Wish lists the whole family can see

Giftster, GiftList and similar apps all do the same job: one shared list where relatives can tick something off without the others seeing. It takes ten minutes to set up and saves two duplicate presents every single year.

6What to set up on an iPhone first

Turn on Screen Time and block in-app purchases. Seasonal apps are exactly the kind where something gets bought by accident, and several of the apps above sell items between $5 and $20.

For a child’s account under Family Sharing, switch on “Ask to Buy”. And check one thing for every app you consider: does it want a photo of your child? That is a piece of data you do not get back.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best Santa app for iPhone?

It depends what you want. For a personal call where you decide what Santa says, Call from Santa is built for exactly that. For the biggest production with the most scenarios, Portable North Pole. For Santa on screen, Video Call Santa. For free tracking on Christmas Eve, NORAD.

Are Santa apps free?

All of them are free to download and all of them charge inside the app. The models differ: subscriptions, episode packs, per-call pricing, or per-message. Portable North Pole publishes no price at all until checkout.

Which Santa apps ask for a photo of your child?

Portable North Pole, Message from Santa and Personalized Call from Santa all ask for one according to their own store listings. Call from Santa does not.

What age are Santa call apps for?

Roughly three to nine. Below that a child does not follow the call; above it most children are already working it out for themselves.
