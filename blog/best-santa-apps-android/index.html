Best Santa and Christmas apps
for Android in 2026

From a personal call from Santa to the NORAD tracker and the family wish list. What is actually worth installing from the Play Store this December, what it costs, and what to check before you hand over the phone.

1A phone call from Santa

The category that lands hardest, because it speaks to your child instead of to the occasion. Four are worth knowing about on Android, and they differ mainly in how much you decide.

Ours

Call from Santa

Santa calls your child personally. He says their name, he knows what is on the wish list, and he knows what they have been up to that week.

You put the call together yourself: for every part you pick from suggestions or write your own. You listen to the whole message back before your child hears it, and you decide when the phone rings: now, with thirty seconds to get the handset ready, or later that evening.

First three messages free, then 3 for €1.99, 7 for €2.99 or 15 for €3.99. No subscription and no forced ads. We never ask for a photo of your child.

Full disclosure: we make this one, a small team working evenings. That is why it is flagged rather than quietly slipped in at number one.

Portable North Pole

Ten million downloads and a 4.6 from more than half a million Play Store reviews, updated as recently as this month. More than forty scenarios, over fifty scenes, and a live “Talk to Santa”. Their help centre states they run no third-party advertising. Two things to know: no price is published anywhere on their own site, and the Play listing says Santa uses your child’s name, age and photo.

Video Call Santa

Worth comparing across platforms before you install: 4.7 on iPhone, but 3.5 on Google Play, where the listing also says “Contains ads”. You choose from 46 pre-recorded episodes and pick a name from a list; individual names cost $1.99.

Message from Santa

Five million downloads and the widest feature set for the least money, with a yearly plan at $9.99. It is also the one with “Contains ads” on Play and an in-app purchase called “Remove Ads”. The videos are a choice of three ready-made ones with your child’s name and photo added, and the chat is, in their own words, “simulated and powered by AI”.

On the left you put the message together and listen back, on the right the phone rings.

2Tracking Santa on Christmas Eve

The NORAD Santa Tracker has been running since 1955, it is free, and it has no catch. Put it on around five o’clock on the 24th and let the children check between courses.

It also hands you the only bedtime argument that has ever worked: he will not come while you are awake.

3Christmas music and the countdown

You do not need a dedicated app for this. Spotify and YouTube Music both run Christmas stations from late November. Download one playlist for offline use, because the weakest link in Christmas Eve is the wifi at someone else’s house.

4Wish lists the whole family can see

Giftster, GiftList and similar apps all do the same job: one shared list where relatives can tick something off without the others seeing. Ten minutes to set up, and it saves two duplicate presents every year.

5How to read a Play Store listing before you install

Three things tell you most of what you need. Contains ads under the title. The Data safety section, which says what is collected, what is shared with third parties, and whether you can have it deleted. And the last updated date: a Christmas app that has not been touched for two years will have the same bugs it had then.

6Stop accidental purchases before December starts

In the Play Store, open Settings, then Authentication, and require a password for every purchase. Several of the apps above sell items between $5 and $20, and a child tapping through a Santa app will find them.

With Family Link you can block purchases entirely on your child’s device. And check one thing for every app: does it want a photo of your child? That is a piece of data you do not get back.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best Santa app for Android?

It depends what you want. For a personal call where you decide what Santa says, Call from Santa is built for exactly that. For the biggest production, Portable North Pole. For free tracking on Christmas Eve, NORAD.

Which Santa apps on Android contain ads?

Video Call Santa and Message from Santa are both listed with “Contains ads” on Google Play, and Message from Santa sells a “Remove Ads” purchase. Call from Santa has no forced ads.

Which Santa apps ask for a photo of your child?

Portable North Pole and Message from Santa both ask for one according to their own listings. Call from Santa does not.

How do I stop my child buying something by accident?

Require a password for every purchase in the Play Store settings, or block purchases entirely through Family Link on your child’s device.
